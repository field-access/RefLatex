ConceptCards

Your ideas, in view.

A visual workspace for Markdown notes and mind maps. Bring your study material onto one canvas, see how it fits together, and focus on one idea at a time.

  • 01

    Bring your notes together

    Import Markdown files or paste text to build a canvas of editable cards.

  • 02

    Read it. Map it.

    Switch the same note between a reading card and a mind map. Keep equations, code and tables alongside your ideas.

  • 03

    See the whole picture

    Find a topic through search or the card index, zoom out for an overview, then give a card room to read.

  • 04

    Make a workspace for each subject

    Save separate canvases, reopen recent work, and export an individual card as a PDF.
